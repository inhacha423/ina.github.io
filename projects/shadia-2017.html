Instead of using drones or existing satellite images, we wanted students to be involved in making the maps themselves. They carried balloons with small action cameras, took photographs, and pieced the images together to map their neighborhoods. - type: image image: /assets/projectimages/shadia-2017/detail-02.png alt: "Map knitting process: balloon making, aerial photography, and assembling photographs" width: 2098 height: 10791 - type: text text: >- As students worked on their maps, they identified familiar places, including their homes, schools, and nearby streets, and shared stories about them. These activities sparked conversations about their neighborhoods and how they saw the places around them. The project was not just about making maps, but also about what we could learn through the process of making them. - type: image image: /assets/projectimages/shadia-2017/detail-03.png alt: "Completed school maps, presentations, and photographs of the Shadia project" width: 2099 height: 5242 ---
